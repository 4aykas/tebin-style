# Quick start — no terminal required

Everything here is a link you click and a value you type. Nothing to install.

## 1. Get the logo

Open the asset table in
[TEBIN Classic — DESIGN.md](https://github.com/4aykas/tebin-style/blob/main/themes/tebin-classic/DESIGN.md)
and click the size you need:

| You are making | Take |
|---|---|
| Email signature, small web use | **512 px** |
| A Word document or a slide | **1024 px** |
| Anything that will be printed | **2048 px** |

The white logo comes **on a red tile and on a charcoal tile**, never on
transparency — a white logo on a transparent background looks like an empty
file in every preview. The tiles already carry the correct clear space, so you
can place them as they are.

## 2. Get the colours

The two colours that matter most:

| Colour | HEX | RGB (what Office asks for) |
|---|---|---|
| Brand red | `#DA291C` | 218, 41, 28 |
| Brand grey | `#898D8D` | 137, 141, 141 |

The full palette, ready for Excel:
[colors.csv](https://github.com/4aykas/tebin-style/blob/main/themes/tebin-classic/dist/colors.csv?raw=1).

## 3. Which theme?

- **TEBIN Classic** — anything that must match printed TEBIN material:
  documents, certificates, slide decks, business cards.
- **TEBIN** — web work in the current tebin.pro style (charcoal, signal red).
- **Slate** — a neutral starter for projects that are not TEBIN-branded.

Each theme's `DESIGN.md` is a single self-contained page:
[tebin-classic](https://github.com/4aykas/tebin-style/blob/main/themes/tebin-classic/DESIGN.md) ·
[tebin](https://github.com/4aykas/tebin-style/blob/main/themes/tebin/DESIGN.md) ·
[slate](https://github.com/4aykas/tebin-style/blob/main/themes/slate/DESIGN.md)

## 4. The four rules that matter outside a browser

1. On dark or red backgrounds use the **all-white** logo; the red-and-grey
   version is for light backgrounds only.
2. Keep clear space around the logo of at least **the height of the "B"**.
3. Never stretch, shadow or recolor the logo.
4. The corner mark may stand alone — top-right of a photo or slide — to mark
   TEBIN authorship.
